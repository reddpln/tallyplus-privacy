Privacy Architecture

1. Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026 • Governing Law: Republic of the Philippines (RA 10173)

At tAlly+, your intimate wellness and personal privacy are our highest priority. The application is built on a local-first philosophy with zero advertising, zero cross-app tracking, and no monetization of intimate health data.

1.1 Local-First Storage by Default

  • All encounters, partner directories, STI screening records, test results, notes, and medication schedules are stored directly on your physical iPhone.
  • We do not run background telemetry, analytics trackers, or commercial tracking servers.

1.2 Zero Advertising & No Sale of Data

  • tAlly+ contains zero third-party advertising SDKs and zero cross-app trackers.
  • We never sell, broker, lease, or monetize your intimate records, sexual orientation, partner names, or health history to any data broker, insurer, advertiser, or commercial entity.

1.3 Optional Cloud Backup (Google Firebase)

  • Cloud Backup is 100% optional. Only if you choose to sign in with Apple, Google, or Email are your records uploaded to Google Firebase.
  • What is uploaded: your encounters, partners, test results (including attached lab-result images), medications, reminders, notes, and profile.
  • Backups are protected by HTTPS/TLS encryption in transit and Google Cloud encryption at rest. Backups are not end-to-end encrypted.
  • By enabling Cloud Backup, you give your explicit consent to this upload. You may permanently delete your cloud account and all synced backups at any time in Settings.

1.4 Device Hardware Permissions

  • Photo Library & Camera: Used only when you choose to attach an avatar or lab-result image. Photos remain stored on your device (and included in cloud backup if enabled).
  • Face ID & Touch ID: Used exclusively for local on-device biometric app unlock. Biometric data never leaves Apple's Secure Enclave.
  • Local Notifications: Scheduled directly on your device for medication and screening reminders. No notification payloads are routed through our servers.

1.5 Your Rights & Data Sovereignty

  • Export Data: Generate an unencrypted JSON export of your records at any time in Settings.
  • Erase Local Data: Permanently wipe all local records, passcodes, and security keys using "Erase All Local Data" in Settings.
  • Delete Cloud Account: Permanently delete your cloud account and all synced backups in Settings > Account & Cloud Backup > Delete Cloud Account.
  • Under the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173) and applicable global laws, you have rights to access, correct, erase, and object to processing, or lodge inquiries with the National Privacy Commission.

1.6 Age Restriction & Children's Privacy

tAlly+ is intended solely for adults aged 18 and older. It is not directed to minors, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18.

The contents, countdowns, test cadences, and reminder tools in this application are for personal organizational reference only and do NOT constitute medical advice, clinical diagnosis, treatment, or medical recommendations.

3.2 Always Consult Licensed Healthcare Providers

Always seek the guidance of a licensed physician, STI clinic, or qualified medical professional with any questions regarding sexual health, screening intervals, symptoms, test interpretations, or clinical treatments. Never disregard or delay seeking professional clinical guidance because of something you have logged, calculated, or read in tAlly+.

3.3 Medication Scheduling (PrEP, PEP, Doxy-PEP)

Information and reminder presets for Pre-Exposure Prophylaxis (PrEP), Post-Exposure Prophylaxis (PEP), Doxycycline Post-Exposure Prophylaxis (Doxy-PEP), or antibiotics are scheduling aids only. Follow your prescribing physician's or dispensing pharmacist's instructions exactly regarding dosage, timing, and administration.

Emergency PEP Notice: Post-exposure prophylaxis (PEP) is time-critical. If you suspect an exposure to HIV or an STI, contact an emergency room or sexual health clinic immediately, without delay.
